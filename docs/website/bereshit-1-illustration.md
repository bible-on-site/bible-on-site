# בראשית א illustration pilot

The illustration is an editorial interpretation of Genesis 1. It does not depict God, claim to reconstruct a historical scene, or replace the chapter text and Tanahpedia references.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then visually inspected and encoded from the generated PNG. The displayed image has a Hebrew alt description, a visible interpretive caption, and a nearby textual summary.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: editorial hero illustration for a Hebrew Bible website chapter page, Genesis 1 (בראשית א), intended for responsive website use and social sharing. Create one wide landscape composition with crisp detailed rendering suitable for a 16:9 crop.
Primary request: a reverent, interpretive visual summary of the creation account in Genesis 1. A unified panoramic scene: light emerging over primordial waters; clear sky above and sea below; dry land and flourishing plants in the foreground; sun, moon, and stars visible in the heavens; a few birds in flight and fish visible beneath the water. Make the scene coherent rather than a numbered sequence or infographic.
Style/medium: refined contemporary painterly editorial illustration with subtle paper texture and elegant natural forms, not a photorealistic reconstruction. Deep indigo and blue balanced with luminous gold and fresh green. Strong central focal point and enough visual breathing room for responsive crops.
Constraints: no text, Hebrew letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, maps, timelines, family trees, borders, or labels. No depiction of God or a divine human figure. No invented buildings or historical artifacts. Do not add article UI or mockup framing. No embedded typography. This is editorial interpretation, not a claim of historical evidence.
```
