## What is compared

The shipped מסורה division derives reading units from `ptuha`/`stuma`
parasha markers in the Sefaria corpus (see docs/data/reading-units.md).
The user-supplied reference — `חלוקת ה"סדרים" של המסורה`,
[prakim.pdf](https://www.tanachyomi.co.il/PDFFiles/prakim.pdf) — lists the
first pasuk of every Masoretic seder in Neviim and Ketuvim, numbered per
תנ"ך קורן. **סדרים and פתוחה/סתומה are different Masoretic systems and
are not expected to coincide**: literature documents seder heads placed
at a parasha's start, at its last pasuk, and inside it (חלוקת התנ"ך
לסדרים, Hebrew Wikipedia). This report therefore classifies every listed
seder head instead of expecting a 1:1 match.

- Parenthesized seder numbers mark boundaries attested only in some ancient manuscripts; they are excluded from the numbered sequence.

## Classification detail

system difference** = a mid-parasha seder head documented in masoretic
literature; **manuscript-only** = parenthesized boundary not in the numbered
tradition; **edition divergence** = the corpus parasha shape differs from the
documented edition; **needs adjudication** = consistent with the two-system
divergence, but lacking entry-specific evidence.
