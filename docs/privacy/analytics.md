# Analytics decision - 22 October 2026 (#2400)

Scope: Israeli-operated, Hebrew learning service aimed at Israel; operator confirms AWS hosting in Israel. No accounts or login. This does not make technical identifiers anonymous, nor place external providers' processing in Israel.

## Applicable requirement

The [Privacy Protection Law, amended by Amendment 13](https://main.knesset.gov.il/APPS/legislation/main/laws/2000234) permits informed express or implied consent (§3). Amendment 13 expands personal information to identifiable persons/online identifiers and strengthens enforcement; it does not create a universal cookie-accept button. Notice duties (§11), purpose limits (§8) and access/correction rights (§§13-14) remain.

The final PPA statement of 25 February 2026, §42, accepts continued browsing after entry disclosure where processing reasonably relates to the service. Silence alone is insufficient (§44); express consent is recommended (§45), not universally mandatory. This is regulator interpretation, not a new blanket statutory opt-in rule. [Official landing page](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/consent-2026); [read final primary document mirrored by EBN](https://www.ebnlaw.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/%D7%92%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-2026.pdf). Official hosting returned 403; the dated final regulator document, not the consultation draft, was read at the mirror.

This preserves useful measurement. It does not establish a categorical exemption for every possible analytics integration. [Google Analytics terms §7](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/us/) require disclosure and storage consent where law requires it; Firebase GA4 uses those terms (§17). [Google EU consent policy](https://www.google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy/) has geographic/product conditions, not an Israeli universal banner rule. Incidental accessibility abroad alone is not evidence that this Israel-directed service targets foreign markets. International targeting, advertising, unrelated profiling, sensitive data or materially new recipients/purposes require a fresh applicability review before enabling them; this release does not add them or an international geolocation system.

The notice does not invent a verified retention period or promise Israel-only processing. Retention depends on data/settings/provider; update the maintained disclosure when configuration changes.

## Rollout and search presentation

At the owner's explicit direction the short-lived rollout key analyticsConsent is retired and ignored. New preferences use a fresh key; continued use is recorded separately from explicit permission. Fresh off choices are respected across revisits. Treating dismissal as a subsequent action after disclosure is an implementation inference, not wording quoted from §42; it is recorded as notice-seen, never as express consent. Policy controls change measurement directly without reopening a prompt.

The entry disclosure and legal section have data-nosnippet from initial rendering. [Google's documented behavior](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots-meta-tag) excludes these fragments from snippets; this is not fragment noindex, a ranking guarantee, or universal crawler support. Scripture/article pages receive no page-wide noindex/nosnippet. The existing privacy fragment remains accessible and indexable as part of its page; no separate legal URL is added. The global head/footer link uses [RFC 6903 privacy-policy](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6903.html#section-4), which identifies a policy and certifies nothing. These documented mechanisms provide no general search-engine compliance declaration. No denied-analytics cookieless pings are introduced; advertising consent defaults are disabled while service statistics remain available.
