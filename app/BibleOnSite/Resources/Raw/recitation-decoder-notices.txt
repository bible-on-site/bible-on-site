The recitation decoder includes mpg123-decoder 1.0.3 and its locked dependencies.
It is bundled from their unchanged ESM implementation, using esbuild 0.28.1.
No codec source has been modified. The app's JS bridge and bundle build source
are available in this repository. Users may rebuild and replace the decoder.

Source and build instructions:
https://github.com/eshaz/wasm-audio-decoders/tree/8f2428c1cd96b54dab74836c8471ff75fe35cbee
https://github.com/madebr/mpg123/tree/08247b317163175e62035893af3ff9e71a5dfefd
https://github.com/bible-on-site/bible-on-site/blob/master/web/bible-on-site/scripts/build-native-recitation-decoder.mjs
