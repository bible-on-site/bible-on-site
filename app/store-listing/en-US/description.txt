Read the Tanakh (also spelled Tanach), the Hebrew Bible, one chapter at a time with the 929 daily reading cycle. Explore the complete Hebrew text alongside classic commentaries, rabbinic articles and recorded recitation.

Designed for Hebrew readers: scripture, commentaries and articles are in Hebrew.

Features:
- Biblical text with vowel marks and cantillation.
- Classic commentaries alongside the verses.
- Recorded chapter recitation with verse and word highlighting.
- Rabbinic articles on the chapter and weekly Torah portion.
- Tanakh-pedia: biblical people, places and concepts.
- A reading calendar and easy chapter navigation.
- Search for verses, chapters and articles.

Free to use, with no advertisements.
